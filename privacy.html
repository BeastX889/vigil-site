Vigil privacy policy

LumioLab · Effective 4 October 2026

Vigil is an always-on display: when your screen turns off, it shows a clock on the lock screen. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

No data collection

Vigil has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. LumioLab receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Settings

Your choices (clock face, colours, fonts and so on) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn on notification access, Vigil reads only the icons of apps that currently have notifications, and the title and artist of media that is playing (so it can show them with play/pause controls). It never reads the text of messages or notifications. This information is used on your phone, in the moment, to draw the screen. It is not stored, logged or shared.

Background photo

If you choose a background photo, Vigil copies that one image into its own private storage on your phone so it can show it behind the clock. Removing the photo in Vigil deletes the copy.

Permissions

Notification access (optional)Notification icons and now-playing, as described above.

Children

Vigil is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
